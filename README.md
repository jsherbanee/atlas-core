# Atlas

Atlas is a commercial Software-as-a-Service platform for Audio Visual and Lighting Systems Integrators.

It is designed as an Intelligent Lifecycle Solutions Management Platform for organizations that design, estimate, procure, deploy, commission, maintain, and service commercial and residential AV and lighting systems.

Atlas is vendor-neutral, company-agnostic, and multi-tenant. It manages operational truth while accounting systems manage financial truth.

Your knowledge stays yours. See docs/PRIVACY_AND_DATA_OWNERSHIP.md and docs/AI_PRIVACY_POLICY.md for the customer-ownership and AI privacy commitments.

Atlas's top-level trust philosophy is documented in docs/TRUST_CHARTER.md.
